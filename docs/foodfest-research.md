# Passua Bites FoodFest research notes

Research date: 2026-08-15 UTC.

## Verified event details

The likely second FoodFest appearance this year was the **Nairobi Street Food Festival — 11th Edition: Playtime Edition**. Public event-guide details identify the event as a food-and-drinks event held on **Saturday, August 8, 2026**, at **ASK Arena / Jamhuri Showground, Main Arena**, from approximately **8:00 AM to 10:00 PM**. The guide describes the experience as good food, music, games, and a day out, with tickets from KSh 500. Source: https://nairobieventsguide.com/event/nairobi-street-food-festival-playtime-edition/

Capital FM’s post-event report, dated August 11, 2026, independently confirms that the Playtime Edition took place at ASK Showground/ASK Arena on Saturday, August 8. It describes an enthusiastic crowd, local street-food vendors, chefs, multicultural food, interactive games, local DJs, Nairobi street style, and a headline performance by Njerae. It specifically frames the event as a lively outdoor marketplace and community hangout. Source: https://capitalfm.africa/flavor-fashion-and-games-why-the-nairobi-street-food-festival-was-the-ultimate-weekend-hangout/

## Safe website wording

Use verified facts: “Nairobi Street Food Festival — Playtime Edition,” “8 August 2026,” “Jamhuri/ASK Showground, Main Arena,” and that Passua Bites joined the vendor lineup with its street-food stall. The site may describe the team’s own experience as a dry, high-energy day with food, music, games, and community, based on the user’s direct confirmation that this edition did not rain and the independent event report’s atmosphere description. Avoid claiming attendance numbers, sales figures, awards, sponsors, or exact team menu items unless the team provides those details or a public source verifies them.
