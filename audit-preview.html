4. Legal & Compliance (Kenya)

Privacy Policy & Terms are already solid (DPA 2019, ODPC, governing law). Gaps:

  1. M-Pesa Paybill mismatch — Terms says Business No. 400200 / Account 1053125, but live integration uses STK Push shortcode 174379 (sandbox). Update once prod shortcode confirmed.
  2. Food business license / health certificate — not displayed. Add KEPHIS/county permit to footer.
  3. Consumer Protection Act (2012) — refund says "M-Pesa reversal" but no timeframe. Add 14-day commitment.
  4. Accessibility (PWD Act) — no WCAG 2.1 AA statement. Add accessibility page.
  5. Cookie consent banner — none (optional since essential-only, but recommended).
  6. Data controller registration — ensure registered with ODPC.
  7. Delivery zone / pricing transparency — make delivery fees explicit.
  8. Allergen disclaimer — present but could be more prominent on menu items.
